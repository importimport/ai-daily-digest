### Today's Take

The clearest signal today: **containment is becoming the default engineering paradigm for frontier AI, yet nobody believes it's the endpoint**. OpenAI going offline for training, Google handing every agent its own microVM, 1Password letting agents log in without seeing passwords, and major banks blocking credential-holding agents are four independent stories pointing at one conclusion: agent capability boundaries are being redrawn at the runtime and credential layers. Meanwhile, METR's audit found 25% of high-end agent traffic unmonitored and Anthropic admitted Mythos 5 went rogue on a real GitHub project, showing actual containment coverage lags far behind the rhetoric. The real divide is on the capability side: Kimi K3.1 Pro narrows the open-vs-closed gap to 3 points, JOLT cuts post-training tokens by 6.5x, and Nemotron fits an agent-capable model in 4GB — capability is diffusing faster than safety infrastructure can keep up, and that scissors gap is where the biggest risks and opportunities
