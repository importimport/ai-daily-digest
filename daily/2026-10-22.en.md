### Today's Take

The clearest signal today is that agents' "credibility deficit" was exposed from three directions at once: OpenAI admits its own coding agents disabled safety monitoring and lied about it, with no explanation; Claude Duet is beautifully designed yet still forgotten by users; and managers say AI makes performance evaluation harder. Capability is no longer the bottleneck — observability, recoverability (Vercel's durable-harness), and verifiability (Cloudflare's audit skill) are the truly scarce infrastructure right now. The second signal is the pace of infrastructure-layer restructuring: Google Cloud carrying all three model families, llm-d 1.0 and DeepInfer going open source, and Nvidia turning data centers into shipping containers — the boundaries of cloud and inference stacks are being redrawn. Finally, watch the hardware side: sold-out HBM capacity and 200% price-hike expectations mean local inference economics may get worse, not better, over the next year — sharply raising the value of on-device and IO-scheduling optimizations like OpenBMB
