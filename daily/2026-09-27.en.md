## 📄 Papers

- **Learning to Discover Interesting Mathematics**: The paper defines a theorem's intrinsic interestingness as the ratio of proof length to statement length, pushing LLMs beyond proving theorems toward finding ones worth proving.

  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.29845)
