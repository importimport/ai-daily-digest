💡 Today's Take

Today's clearest signal is that capital and capability are both entering a convergence phase: OpenAI turning to dentists and other individual investors, Anthropic cutting its IPO valuation, and Ramp data showing flat enterprise AI spending all point to one fact—the AI revenue curve can no longer be carried by narrative. The technical side runs the opposite way: METR's report shows Anthropic's model improvement rate doubling, recursive self-improvement now has empirical grounding, and open models trail closed ones by only 8 points in cyber capability, so the defender's window is genuinely narrowing. The most notable middle layer is the explosion of the "Agent Skills" ecosystem, which is turning agent capabilities from one-off prompts into distributable, auditable—and poisonable—software assets; Cloudflare's audit skill and the AGENTS.MD security warning are two sides of the same coin.
