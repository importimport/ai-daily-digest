📄 Papers

EgoX: Egocentric cross-embodiment manipulation with embodiment dreaming
Organizes heterogeneous demonstrations from humans, real robots, and simulated agents through semantically corresponding observation and action modalities, pretraining a Modularized Cross-Embodiment Transformer to bridge morphology, sensing, and control gaps.
Back to Pixels (PixVid): Lifting latent video diffusion into pixel space
Argues VAE spatiotemporal compression, while making training tractable, now limits high-resolution, per-frame conditioned, and interactive generation, and lifts pretrained latent video models directly to pixel-space generation.
The Value of Thinking: Zero-or-T reasoning in hybrid decision models
A single model either answers implicitly inside one forward pass generating no tokens, or performs full autoregressive reasoning at a cost of T tokens when worth paying — learning when to think rather than how much.
Experience-Handoff RL for recursive self-improvement
Prior work wraps agents in harnesses that refine inputs via reflection or compaction and hands past experience to future rollouts, but construction of handed-off artifacts lacks principle; this work studies how to build and transfer experience for self-improvement.
JustRUA: A controlled study of GPT-6 robot-use agents
Disentangles harness, action interface, experience reuse, and scene geometry in one framework, finding a lean harness captures most of the gains where prior reported systems changed several designs at once.
UniSpark-9B: Exploring hybrid attention for multimodal understanding and generation
Built on a Mixture-of-Transformer-Experts backbone (~9B per branch), combining full attention with GatedDeltaNet linear attention, jointly supporting VQA, text-to-image, and instruction-guided editing via autoregressive text prediction and visual flow matching.
ArchSpace: Recording, reviewing, and referencing LLM architecture research
Addresses how industrial teams (DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi) rarely document which architectural ideas succeeded or failed, and academia is limited to small single-point comparisons, proposing a system to record and cite architecture research.

💡 Today's Take

The clearest signal today is that "decision models" are coalescing into a standalone category: Google's Gemini Decisions API and Perplexity's open-source Decider v1.3 landed the same day, both returning calibrated probabilities instead of text, with pricing pushed into the $0.10-per-million-token range. On the open-source side, llama.cpp reaching 1.0 and AWS shipping an emulator with 30k commits written by an agent swarm in three weeks both show that local-first deployment and agent-written code have crossed the usability threshold.
