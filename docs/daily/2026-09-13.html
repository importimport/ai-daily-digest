📄 Papers

Negative Self-Distillation: learning to reason by avoiding flaws
The work finds that On-Policy Self-Distillation forces students to imitate artificially confident reasoning traces, suppressing genuine reasoning ability; it proposes a negative self-distillation paradigm where models learn from avoiding errors rather than imitating correct answers.
An Open Recipe for IMO Gold: training Nemotron for olympiad mathematics
Starting from Nemotron 3 Ultra, two specialist checkpoints are trained via SFT and RL, and the paper presents an open-model test-time-compute pipeline operating entirely in natural language with no formal prover or external tools.
NCP-ArchPreview: moving towards latent space language models through Next Concept Prediction
Alongside next-token prediction, the model learns to predict discrete concepts spanning multiple tokens, building a product-quantized concept vocabulary and adding an explicit concept-level objective while preserving token-level autoregressive generation.
T1: Terminal Agent Reinforcement Learning for long-horizon tasks
A 122B-total-parameter MoE model operating a real shell in a cloud sandbox for up to 300+ tool-call turns per task, rewarded by each task's own verifier, with a comprehensive training recipe.
SenseNova-U1.5: towards native unified visual intelligence
An 8B-MoT native unified multimodal model that understands, reasons about, and generates visual content within an encoder-free and VAE-free architecture, strengthening its visual interface via spatially coherent patch reconstruction and supporting native resolutions up to 4K.
PARSER: read in parallel, reason in depth for long-context LLM agents
Decoupling reading from reasoning, a bank of lightweight subagents each bound to a single chunk reads the whole document in parallel while a lead agent reasons in depth iteratively, so inference latency no longer scales linearly with document length.
Fly Language Model: wiring the full fruit fly connectome into a frozen 1.2B LLM
It drives all 166,700 retained neurons and 25.6 million edges of the MaleCNS connectome, training only 278,528 parameters; the preprint reports a 0.0222 nat per token gain, but a parameter-matched control without the graph does slightly better in every seed.
AI models' written reasoning steps correspond to distinct internal patterns
Reasoning steps like calculation, formula retrieval, and deduction are clearly separable in a model's internal states, especially in the middle layers — meaning models process more than their visible chain of thought reveals, with direct implications for AI safety.
HyQuant: hybrid-precision quantization for LLM attention
Low-bit quantization of the attention module often introduces large errors at very low bit-widths; HyQuant proposes a hybrid quantization design to better balance accuracy and efficiency rather than relying solely on smoothing techniques for outliers.
Beyond Solver Verdicts: generative reward models for autoformalization
The paper formalizes Verdict-Preserving-Unfaithfulness (VPU) — a failure mode where an incorrect encoding executes successfully and matches the expected verdict — and proves theoretically that structural, verdict-only verification heuristics are mathematically bounded.
