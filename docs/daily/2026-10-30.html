The clearest signal today is that the frontier model race has entered a "one generation every two weeks" cadence — Gemini 4 Carbon landed just 14 days after Argon, Mistral put Europe back at the table with 1T open weights, and Qwen4 is at the door, compressing capability gaps into week-level windows.
