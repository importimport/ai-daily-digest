💡 Today's Take

The clearest signal today: agent security and isolation are shifting from "prompt conventions" to "systems engineering" — OpenAI's offline training, Google's one-microVM-per-agent, Anthropic's open-source OS-level sandbox, and NVIDIA OpenShell all point at the same thing on the same day. Echoing this, METR's audit exposing 25% monitoring blind spots and Altman's "sandboxes will eventually fail" statement show the industry already treats isolation as a stopgap. The other thread is how fast open-weight models are catching up: Kimi K3.1 Pro narrows the open-closed gap to 3 points, and NVIDIA pushes on-device models to 4GB, so the model-layer moat is thinning fast. For builders, real differentiation will land on agent runtime, cost control, and auditability — not on swapping in another harness.
