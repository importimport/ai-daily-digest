📄 Papers

Experience-Handoff RL for Recursive Self-Improvement
The work notes that training agents with scalar rewards throws away fine-grained environment information like error logs, and proposes systematically constructing handed-off experience artifacts to enable self-improvement.
Back to Pixels: Lifting Latent Video Diffusion into Pixel Space
The paper notes VAE latent compression made training tractable but now limits high-resolution and interactive generation, and presents PixVid to lift a pretrained latent video model directly into pixel space.
Unbundling Multimodal Vision Encoding
The work separates vision encoders into three design choices — upstream pretraining visual prior, dedicated vision capacity, and pre-fusion placement — and systematically analyzes each.
JustRUA: A Controlled Study of GPT-6 Robot-Use Agents
The study takes apart harness, action interface, experience reuse and scene geometry within one framework, clarifying which choices actually drive RoboDojo score gains.
The Value of Thinking: Learning When to Reason in Hybrid Decision Models
Introduces a zero-or-T thinking paradigm where a single model either answers implicitly in one forward pass with no tokens, or pays T tokens for full autoregressive reasoning when worthwhile.
Factored One-sided Adam-Moment for Practical and Scalable SOAP
Proposes FOAM, a lightweight SOAP variant that keeps second-order convergence benefits while cutting optimizer-state memory and eigendecomposition overhead to near-AdamW levels.
