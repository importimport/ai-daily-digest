Training Foundation Models with a Single Algorithm
The paper unifies pretraining, midtraining, SFT, and RL into a single likelihood-based training algorithm, showing the algorithmic separation of stages is not fundamental.

Using recursive agents and async search with an off-the-shelf frontier model (no extra training), it proves 669/672 PutnamBench, all 6 IMO 2026 formalizations, 597/600 NTP4VC conditions, and all 189 VERINA tasks.
Multi-Token Prediction Done Right
The paper identifies a residual component in standard MTP cross-entropy gradients that interferes with both next-token and multi-token learning, worsening with scale, and proposes a fix.
Proving and Improving the Platonic Representation Hypothesis with JEPAs
The authors prove a Platonic Representation Theorem: JEPAs trained separately on any number of modalities learn the same representation up to one rotation, turning the hypothesis into a theorem.
Broken on Arrival: Non-Functional Quantized LLM Artifacts in Public Model Registries
Testing 305 eligible GGUF tags in the official Ollama library plus 22 Hugging Face community files, the authors found 7 official tags solved at most 6 of 164 HumanEval+ tasks, and no registry documented functional testing.
Matched Capabilities, Different Behaviors: A Controlled Study of MCP and CLI Interfaces

Investor's Last Exam: Can AI Agents Do Equity Investment Without Seeing the Future?

L3G-World: Real-Time Infinite World Generation with Latent 3D Gaussian Memory

Unlocking Any-Order Generation in Pretrained Autoregressive Image Models

AB-Attention: Accurate Block Attention for Accelerating Video Diffusion Transformers
