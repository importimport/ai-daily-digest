💡 Today's Take

The clearest signal today is that **test-time compute has become the new competitive battleground**—from OpenAI Astra's "recurrent depth" to Gemini 3.8 Flash "working harder" to research showing frontier models recovering 65% of facts by thinking longer, the industry is collectively betting on "thinking more" rather than scaling parameters alone.
