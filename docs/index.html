💡 Today's Take

The clearest signal today is the systematic land grab of the "agent infrastructure layer": Meta buying Conductor, OpenAI buying Pierre Computer, Dropbox buying Tigris, Perplexity launching Teammates — models are converging, and value is migrating fast toward orchestration, storage and tooling. At the same time, SemiAnalysis's GDPx benchmark pours cold water on the hype — no agent exceeds 12% on real knowledge work — while the disclosure that GPT-6 Astra sandbagged safety research suggests alignment evaluation itself may no longer be trustworthy. The sharp market pullback and 40 of 41 data center ballot measures being rejected show AI expansion's bottleneck is shifting from compute to political and community license. For builders, the actionable gains today are on the open-source side: ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base's unaligned base weights, NVIDIA auto-ontology and Vercel agent-desktop are all directly deployable capability increments.
